Profile & settings, many ways

Phone versions of the Profile + Settings screen with everything on it today: photo and name, native language, your 5 languages with track and level, phone colours, daily word goal, hobby, privacy, school, pet, account and plan. The Social group borrows patterns from social language apps (completion ring, streak sheet, tools grid, level dots, edit/preview) and plays its own taps. Each screen scrolls by itself; scroll or tap it to take over (toggles, goals and hobbies respond). Some versions save automatically instead of a big Save button. Example data only.
