LinguaScript Marketing Audit

SEO, buyer's journey, personas (HubSpot GPCT/BA C&I), full-funnel map, email automation and what to automate. Built from the code in the repo and public framework sources. Frameworks used: HubSpot inbound (attract → engage → delight), buyer's journey (awareness → consideration → decision), GPCTBA/C&I.

The verdict

The product is far ahead of the marketing. LinguaScript has a website, Chrome extension, Android app with bubble, widgets and lock-screen card, an MCP connector, Video Studio, Write, Speak, Scan a page and a French course. It has no proof that anyone wants it badly enough to pay, no search presence I could find, and no follow-up for visitors who don't sign up on the spot.

  1. Google can't see you yet. A site:linguascript.co.uk search returned zero pages. Every URL serves the same HTML until JavaScript runs.
  2. No proof. No testimonials, user numbers, ratings or case studies (the landing page says so on purpose: "no invented numbers"). Right call, but it means you need to collect real ones now.
  3. No single ICP. Pages target adults, polyglots, families and schools at once. A solo founder can't run four funnels.
  4. No lead capture and no welcome series. The email system is built for retention (reminders, streaks, recaps). Nothing nurtures a visitor who isn't ready, or turns a new sign-up into an active user in week one.
  5. Too many features, no headline. Visitors need one sentence and one button. Today the story is spread across 20+ pages and 6 test landing pages.

4 · Value proposition and proof

Learn the language from the shows you already watch: every word you save turns red → orange → green on screen, until you understand without subtitles.

Explain it

  • 3-step "how it works" with real screenshots
  • 60-second screen recording: watch → tap → word turns green

Prove it

  • Ask for a quote when a learner hits 100 green words (word_turned_green event already exists)
  • Chrome Web Store rating and count on the home page
  • One "before/after" learner story per month

Compare it

  • Pages: vs LingQ, vs Migaku, vs Duolingo (you have vs Language Reactor)
  • Honest tables: what they do better, too

Lower the risk

  • Free plan stays free; say so in the hero
  • 14-day refund on yearly
  • Founder name and photo on /story
