LinguaScript Partners: your own affiliate system

Replacing GoAffPro with a partner portal inside LinguaScript: every affiliate and marketer gets a login, sees how their audience converts, and gets paid for the sales they bring. Built from your current code (Stripe checkout, payments webhook, experiments, Admin roles).

My view: yes, do it, with three changes

  1. Give partners a "Partner" login, not an "Admin" login. Same dashboard look, but read-only and limited to their own numbers. An admin account can see learners' emails, payments and settings; one leaked affiliate password must never expose that.
  2. Show partners totals, never people. "12 sign-ups from TikTok this week", never "Sophie, 14, from Truro". That keeps you inside UK GDPR without a data agreement with every affiliate.
  3. Start with manual monthly payouts, automate later. Track everything from day one, but pay the first months by bank transfer from a statement. Add Stripe payouts once there are more than a handful of partners.

Done this way it's better than GoAffPro for you: the data stays in your database, partners see the real funnel (not just a click count), and it ties straight into your A/B tests and Site Studio.

Plan, in order

1

Tracking core

  • Tables: partners, referral_clicks, referrals, commissions, payouts.
  • /r/:code link route + ?ref=; partner code saved on sign-up; code field at checkout.
  • Checkout passes partner_id; webhook writes commissions on invoice.paid, reverses on charge.refunded.
  • Done when a test purchase through a test link shows up as a pending commission.
2

Roles and safe numbers

  • Add marketer and partner to app_role.
  • Partners read only through database functions that return totals for their partner ID and hide groups under 5.
  • Done when a partner account can't read any other partner's or learner's row (test it).
3

Partner portal /partners

  • Tabs: Home, My links, Audience, Landing pages (read-only), What's new, Brand kit, Payouts, Rules. One sentence at the top of each.
  • Link builder per channel (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, other); own brand name, logo and socials (you approve logos before they show anywhere).
  • Loaded only after a partner logs in, so visitors never download it.
4

Team workspace

  • Marketers see all partners, A/B results by partner audience, Site Studio drafts, and the content plan.
  • Invite flow: you add an email + role; they get a sign-in link.
5

Payouts

  • Month 1–3: monthly statement (CSV) and bank transfer from you, minimum £20.
  • Later: Stripe Connect Express. Stripe checks each partner's identity and sends the money; check Stripe's current Connect fees before switching.
  • Then remove GoAffPro from index.html.

Commission: a starting proposal

  • Partners: a share of each payment from their referrals for the first 12 months (for example 20–30%). Simple to explain, fair for subscriptions, and it ends, so your margin recovers.
  • Hold 30 days before a commission can be paid, so refunds and chargebacks don't cost you.
  • Partner code discount (e.g. 10% off the first month) helps conversion and lets codes work on videos and podcasts.
  • These numbers are a starting point, not a benchmark. Check your margin after Stripe fees, AI costs and VAT before you publish them.

Rules to get right before launch

  • Adults only. Partners must be 18+ to be paid (Stripe requires it, and a contract with a minor is a problem). Many of your learners are teens, so they can't be partners.
  • Ads must say they're ads. UK advertising rules (CAP Code, enforced by the ASA) say affiliate posts must be clearly labelled, e.g. "#ad". Put it in the partner terms and the brand kit.
  • Data. Partners see totals only; never names, emails or single people. Update your privacy notice to say referral codes are recorded.
  • Cookies. A 30-day referral cookie needs consent; codes and same-visit sign-ups don't.
  • Tax. Partners are paid as independent contractors and handle their own tax. Keep the statements. Ask an accountant how VAT applies to the commissions you pay.
  • Fraud. No self-referrals, no buying ads on "LinguaScript" searches, no fake accounts. Commissions on refunded payments are reversed.
  • Unreleased features. Partners see only what you're happy to make public. Mark anything else confidential in the terms.
  • Terms. Get a short partner agreement checked by a solicitor before the first payout.
