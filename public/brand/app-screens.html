How we roll this out

From picture to real app

1) You pick one theme from the journey strip. 2) That theme becomes a design system in the code (colours, type, cards, buttons, motion). 3) Screens get rebuilt one at a time in this order: onboarding → home → player → flashcards → reviews → leaderboard → profile. Every step is tested on your phone before the next.
