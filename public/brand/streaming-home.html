Conversion audit

What makes a streaming home convert

01Continue watching firstNetflix and YouTube both put "pick up where you left off" at the top. For us it's also the fastest way to the next saved word.
02One big "play" actionThe hero should have one obvious button. Every extra choice slows people down (Hick's law).
03"% you understand" on every posterOur unique hook. No other streaming app tells you how much of a film you'll understand. It turns choosing into a game.
04Personal rails by hobby"Football in French" beats "Popular". The onboarding hobby should drive the second row.
05Today's goal always visibleA small progress bar (3 / 10 words) creates the pull to watch one more clip.
06Gold reviews as a reward, not a choreA small gold pill ("32 ready ✨") in the header: one tap, back to watching after.
07Platforms as doors, not distractionsYouTube stays the main hub; Netflix, Crunchyroll and TV5MONDE are clear buttons that open with the extension, not equal tabs.
08Paste a link, anywhereA search/paste bar in the header handles "I already know what I want to watch".

Highest-converting bets to test first: #2 Netflix-style hero (familiar = easy), #4 Platform launcher (makes the extension obvious) and #11 Understand-first (our unique angle).
