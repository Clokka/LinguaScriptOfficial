Colour theory

What each colour says

Black #050607
Black: cinema, focus, premiumFeels like a dark cinema: your content glows. Luxury brands use it to say "serious and expensive". Our base.
Green #34C759
Green: growth, "I know this"Go, success, progress. Our hero colour: a word turning green is the whole promise. Use it for the main button.
Gold #FFD54A
Gold: reward, mastery, valueTrophies and premium. Rare = precious, so keep it to one gold section per page: reviews, Pro, achievements.
Red #FF3B30
Red: new, attention, energyGrabs the eye fastest. For us it means "new word", not "error". Keep it small: only on words.
Orange #FF8A00
Orange: warm, in progressFriendly and energetic, sits between new and known. Good for streaks and "keep going" moments.
Off-white #F4F6F5
White: clarity, trustText on dark, and the colour of the cleanest buttons. A split black/white feels editorial and confident.
Violet glow #4C40DC
Violet: night, creativity, AIYour current site's glow. It reads "AI startup", which is why it feels generic. Use it only as a faint glow, or swap it for green or gold.
Black + goldLuxury, achievement. The "gold to black" contrast you like. Best for reviews, Pro and the hero.
Black + greenGrowth and tech. Calm and confident. Best everyday app look.
Black + whiteEditorial, bold, honest. Great for landing pages and ads.
Glass over colourModern Apple feel. Glass needs colour behind it to look like glass.
Red orange green goldOnly on words. The deck colours are meaning, not decoration, so never use them as background colours.

Rule of thumb (60-30-10): about 60% black, 30% one neutral (cards, text), 10% accent: green for actions, with one small gold moment per screen.
