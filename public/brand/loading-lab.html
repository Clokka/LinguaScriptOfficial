Fetching and translating subtitles takes 10–30 seconds. Here are 12 complete loading experiences, 100 loading screens (20 LinguaScript animations × 5 frame styles) and 30 motion graphics, mostly the "loading → playing" moment. All live, all in your colours and chameleon.

Rules for a 10–30 second wait
Show real progress, in steps"Finding subtitles → Translating → Colouring your words". A wait with visible steps feels shorter than a spinner.
Give an honest time estimate"About 15 seconds" beats silence. Under-promise: say 20 if it's usually 15.
Teach while they waitShow 3–5 words from this video ("you'll hear…"). The wait becomes a pre-lesson, and those words light up in the film.
Start the video soonerPlay the video as soon as it's ready and let subtitles colour in when they arrive. Cache every subtitle file so the second visit is instant.
End with a revealA satisfying transition (letterbox opening, colour wash) turns "finally" into "wow".
Never just a spinner after 3 sAfter ~3 seconds, people need something to read or do.
