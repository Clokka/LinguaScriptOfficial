LinguaScript turns any YouTube video with subtitles into a language lesson. Here’s how it works in three quick steps.

Jevoudraisuncafé
I would like a coffee
café coffee

  1. Open a YouTube video

    Pick a video in the language you’re learning. LinguaScript shows its subtitles with your own language underneath.

  2. Tap any word

    See what it means, with examples, and save it to your flashcards with one tap. Try it on the glowing word above.

  3. Watch the colours change

    Every word is coloured by your flashcard decks, so you can see your progress while you watch.
