# Four Pillars

**1. RETENTION (Duolingo / Slidebean)**
- Streak = sunk-cost identity. Users fear losing who they've become, not XP.
- Loss aversion > gain motivation. Design around protecting what users already have.
- Streak shield doubles as monetisation + retention.
- Optimise for daily opens over long sessions. End every session on an open loop.
- "Streak at risk" push beats achievement push 3×.
- LinguaScript apply: sad-pet streak-at-risk push at 20h; paywall at moment of friction (limit hit), not app entry; end session with "X words from unlocking [pet animation]".

**2. HABIT DESIGN (Nir Eyal / Hooked)**
- Hook = Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment. We have Action + Variable Reward; **Investment is our gap**.
- Every session must increase cost of leaving. Saved words, pet bond, streak = investment deposits.
- External triggers (push/email) must graduate to internal triggers ("I feel bored → I open LinguaScript").
- Users who invest DATA (vocab list, pet name, progress) churn exponentially less.
- LinguaScript apply: after 3 saved words → "You've started a collection — review before you go?". Pet IS the investment mechanism — bonded pet makes leaving feel like abandonment. After 10+ sessions measure push open rate; internal trigger should be forming.

**3. NEUROSCIENCE (Huberman — dopamine)**
- Variable reward schedules spike dopamine MORE than guaranteed rewards. Unpredictability is the engine of craving.
- Dopamine peaks BEFORE reward, not during — anticipation is the hook.
- Reward every session = desensitisation. Space out milestone moments.
- Avoid "dopamine flatline" — not every watch session should feel identical.
- LinguaScript apply: never give XP in round numbers (47 > 50). Show milestone progress bars MID-session not at start. Occasional unannounced "Rare Word!" badge for words appearing in <1% of videos. Pavlovian sound loop on word save.

**4. GAMIFICATION (Extra Credits — Skinner Box)**
- Variable ratio > fixed ratio > variable interval > fixed interval for reinforcement strength.
- Slot machines, Pokémon, Duolingo XP all use VR schedules — same mechanism.
- Warning: hollow/manipulative if reward has no intrinsic value.
- Ethical anchor: every LinguaScript reward IS real learning value (saved word genuinely useful).
- LinguaScript apply: random "double XP" moments ~1 in 8 sessions, unannounced. Pet plays surprise animation ~1 in 8 sessions with no trigger. Occasional "rare word bonus" keeps clicking behaviour alive.

# Retention Playbook (mapped triggers)

| Moment | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|
| User finishes first video | Open-loop prompt: "You saved 3 words — review before you go?" | Nir Eyal |
| User hits 10-save daily limit | Show /upgrade paywall at friction; pet reacts to being blocked | Slidebean |
| 2 words from a pet milestone | Animated progress bar mid-session only | Huberman |
| 20 hrs since last session | Push: sad pet + "your streak is at risk" (loss aversion) | Slidebean |
| ~1 in 8 sessions (random) | Surprise pet bonus animation + double XP (variable reward) | Skinner Box |
| After 10th session | Stop pushing — measure open rate (internal trigger forming) | Nir Eyal |
