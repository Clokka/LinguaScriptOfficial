# LinguaScript Learner Survey (Google Forms)

`linguascript-survey.gs` builds the whole form automatically.

1. Open https://script.google.com and click **New project**.
2. Delete what's there, paste in all of `linguascript-survey.gs`, then click **Save**.
3. Pick `createLinguaScriptSurvey` and click **▶ Run**. Allow access when Google asks.
4. Open **Execution log**. It shows the **share link** and the **edit link**. The form is also saved in your Google Drive.

13 questions in 5 sections: About you · Your background · Other apps · LinguaScript and you · Your feedback.
For HubSpot, copy the same questions and answer choices into a HubSpot feedback form.

## Extra data that makes answers more useful
- **Learner stage:** new visitor, free user, paid user, or a user who stopped (send the link with `?stage=`, or add a hidden field).
- **Target language and level** (e.g. French A1): beginners and advanced learners need different things.
- **Platform:** web, Chrome extension, or Android app.
- **How active they are:** days used per week and words saved, taken from our own database rather than asking them.
- **Where they signed up from:** referral partner code or `from=` link (e.g. the words pages).
- **School / Classroom user or not.**
- **Region and native language**, for app-store and translation priorities.
